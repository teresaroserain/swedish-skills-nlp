# Data and license checklist

Do not make this folder public until the authors have confirmed all items below.

- Confirm that Arbetsformedlingen job-ad text may be redistributed, not merely
  downloaded and analyzed.
- Confirm that the expert annotations and prompt-development files may be
  released under the intended repository license.
- Confirm that parsed commercial-model outputs may be redistributed under the
  relevant provider terms and project consent.
- Review advertisements for personal names, email addresses, telephone numbers,
  URLs, and other personal data before release.
- Replace or remove author workstation paths in historical scripts if desired;
  they are provenance, not secrets, but are not portable.
- Add a code license and a separate data license. They need not be the same.
- Document exact model checkpoints and dependency versions available from the
  executed artifacts. Commercial provider snapshots that cannot be recovered
  must remain listed as a limitation.

This package intentionally includes the source workbooks and job-ad text because
the requested local archive was to be complete. Their presence is not a legal
or ethical determination that public redistribution is permitted.
